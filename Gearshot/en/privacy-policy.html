Gearshot Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026

1. Who we are

Gearshot is a game published under the name ADKCH by APPS DEVELOPMENT - KAMIL CHACIAK, a business based in Poland (“we”, “us”). We are the controller of any personal data described in this policy. You can contact us at apps.dev.kch@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • We do not run servers for the game, and we do not collect, receive, or store personal data about you.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device.
  • The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect data from your device to show and measure those ads, as described in section 4.
  • Where the law requires it, you are asked for your consent before ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time.

3. Data stored on your device

The game saves your profiles, progress, and settings in the app's private storage on your device. This data is not sent to us or to any other party by the game. Android cloud backup is turned off for the app. Uninstalling the game deletes this data.

4. Ads and Google

The game shows banner ads and optional ads that you choose to watch. Ads are provided by Google AdMob through Google's Mobile Ads SDK. When an ad is requested, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process, for example:

  • your device's advertising ID, IP address, and approximate location derived from the IP address;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, and app version;
  • information about ad interactions, such as ads shown, clicked, or completed;
  • crash and performance diagnostics.

Google uses this data to deliver ads, to measure and report on them, to prevent fraud and abuse, and, if you consent, to personalise ads. We do not receive this personal data. We receive only aggregated reports about ad performance. For the data it collects for these purposes, Google acts as an independent controller. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

The game needs the following Android permissions: internet access and network state (to load ads), and the advertising ID permission (so that ads can be requested and measured). The Google library may also use related ad-services and wake-lock permissions.

5. Your consent and choices

  • Consent. In regions where the law requires it, such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the game shows Google's consent message before requesting ads. The game does not request ads until the message has been handled where it is required.
  • Changing your choice. If your region requires the consent message, open Settings → Privacy choices in the game at any time to review or withdraw your choice.
  • Android settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads).

6. Legal basis

For users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, storing or accessing information on your device for personalised advertising is based on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. We do not ourselves process personal data about you for any other purpose.

7. Sharing and sale

We do not sell personal data. The only recipient of data from the game's ad requests is Google and its advertising partners, as described in section 4. Google may process data outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area, under the safeguards described in its policies.

8. Retention

Game data on your device is kept until you delete it or uninstall the game. We hold no other personal data about you. Google keeps ad-related data according to its own policies.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal data about you, requests about data collected for ads are best directed to Google through Google My Ad Center and its privacy tools. You can also contact us at the address above and we will help where we can.

You have the right to complain to a supervisory authority. In Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych), uodo.gov.pl.

10. Children

Gearshot is intended for teenagers and adults. It is not directed at children under 13, or under 16 in the European Economic Area, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will help.

11. Security

Ad requests are made over encrypted connections. Game data stays in the app's private storage on your device.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the game changes. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. Material changes will be reflected in the game's next release.
